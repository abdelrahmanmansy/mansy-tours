About this experience

This is the classic Nile journey, done properly. A small, elegant riverboat, a maximum of 24 guests, and stops that actually matter — Philae at sunset, Kom Ombo at golden hour, Edfu before the coaches arrive.

Evenings are for deck chairs and cardamom tea rather than entertainment schedules. Your Egyptologist travels with you, and every shore excursion is included with private transfers.

What makes it special

4 nights in a Nile-view cabin
Philae, Kom Ombo & Edfu temples
Valley of the Kings & Hatshepsut
All meals on board

Your itinerary

  1. 1

    Day 1

    Aswan & Philae Temple

    Embark, settle in, then sail by boat to Philae for the late-afternoon light.

  2. 2

    Day 2

    Kom Ombo

    A morning at the double temple of Sobek and Horus, followed by lunch as we sail north.

  3. 3

    Day 3

    Edfu & the Nile

    Visit the best-preserved temple in Egypt by horse carriage, then a lazy afternoon on deck.

  4. 4

    Day 4

    Luxor's west bank

    Valley of the Kings, Hatshepsut's temple and the Colossi of Memnon.

  5. 5

    Day 5

    Karnak & disembarkation

    Explore Karnak with your guide before transfer to your hotel or the airport.
