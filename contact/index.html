Ask us anything
Do you offer private tours?

Almost every experience can be made private for a small supplement. Tell us who is travelling and we will design the day around you — including a guide in your preferred language.

When is the best time to visit Egypt?

October to April is the most comfortable season for sightseeing. The Red Sea is wonderful year-round, and summer is perfect for diving, early-morning tours and quieter monuments.

How do payments work?

Reserve your dates for free, then pay securely online or in cash on arrival. We never ask for a deposit at the time of booking, and cancellation is free up to 48 hours before departure.

Are airport transfers included?

Private airport transfers are included on all multi-day packages and can be added to any day tour. Our driver meets you inside arrivals with a name board.

Is Egypt safe for families and solo travellers?

Yes. Our guests travel with licensed guides and trusted drivers throughout. We monitor conditions daily, share a 24/7 WhatsApp line and plan every transfer door to door.

Can you accommodate dietary requirements?

Absolutely. Vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free and halal meals are easy to arrange — just mention it when you book and we will brief every restaurant and camp along the way.

Need an answer right now?

Our WhatsApp line is monitored from 8:00 to 22:00 Cairo time.
