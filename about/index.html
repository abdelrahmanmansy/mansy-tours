About us

We are Mansy Tours.

An independent, family-run travel company with one obsession: showing Egypt the way locals see it — slowly, warmly and without a script.

Our story

Founded by a guide,
not a boardroom.

Mansy Tours began in 2011 with Ali Mansy, a licensed Egyptologist from Giza who was tired of watching visitors rushed through the monuments he grew up with. He started with one car, one notebook of trusted drivers and a simple promise: no scripts, no queues, no forty-seat coaches.

Fourteen years later, we are still independent and still family-run. Our guides are Egyptologists, divers, Bedouin trackers and cooks — people who live here, love it and know exactly when to slow down and let a place speak for itself.

We are small on purpose. It means we can change plans when the weather turns, find the quiet corner of a temple, and remember your name on the second day.

What we believe

Travel should feel personal

These are not slogans on a wall. They are the rules our guides are hired to keep.

Journeys, not itineraries

Every route is designed around a feeling — a sunrise alone on the Nile, a family dinner in a Nubian home, the silence of the White Desert.

Small groups, big moments

No coaches, no queues of forty. Groups stay under twelve travellers, and private departures are always an option.

Travel that gives back

We work with family-run hotels, local skippers and artisans — so more of your money stays in the communities you visit.

Cared for, end to end

Airport meet-and-greet, honest advice and a guide who remembers your name. The details are our favourite part.

The long way round

Fourteen years,
one river.

We grew slowly on purpose. Every new route was tested by our own team first, and every guide was chosen for character as much as knowledge.

  1. 2011

    One guide, one car

    Ali Mansy starts running private pyramid days for friends of friends, with a notebook of trusted drivers and a very old Land Cruiser.

  2. 2014

    The first Nile cruise

    We charter our first small riverboat between Aswan and Luxor — 12 guests, one Egyptologist and a cook who still works with us today.

  3. 2018

    A Red Sea base

    Our diving team opens in Hurghada, adding reefs, wrecks and desert camps to the map without losing the small-group ethos.

  4. 2022

    4,000 travellers

    A milestone year. We celebrate with a staff trip to Siwa and a promise to stay independent, whatever happens next.

  5. 2026

    Still curious

    New routes in the Western Desert, a bigger guiding team and the same rule as day one: no scripts, no coaches, no shortcuts.
