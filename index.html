Our story

Local guides. Real Egypt.
No scripts.

Mansy Tours began with one guide, one car and a belief that Egypt deserves to be seen slowly. Fourteen years later we are still independent, still family-run, and still obsessed with the small details that turn a good trip into the one you talk about for years.

Our guides are Egyptologists, divers, Bedouin trackers and cooks — people who grew up here and love showing it off.

  • Handpicked, family-run hotels
  • Private guides, never scripts
  • Small groups under 12
  • 24/7 WhatsApp support
  • Honest, no-pressure pricing
  • Fair pay for local crews
